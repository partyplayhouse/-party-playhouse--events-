1. Activity Acknowledgment

I understand that participation in the Party Playhouse & Events Paint Splatter Room is an active recreational experience that may include paint guns or similar paint-dispensing equipment, paint splattering, target activities, movement around a paint-covered environment, and other related activities. I understand that paint may come into contact with skin, hair, clothing, shoes, protective equipment, and personal belongings.

2. Safety Rules

  • Follow all posted rules and staff instructions.
  • Wear required protective equipment, including eye protection.
  • Use paint guns and other equipment only as instructed.
  • Never intentionally aim or discharge paint toward another participant’s face or head.
  • No running, horseplay, unsafe equipment use, or intentionally creating hazardous conditions.
  • Remain within designated activity areas.
  • Immediately notify staff of spills, damaged equipment, injury, illness, or unsafe conditions.
  • Stop participating if instructed by staff.

3. Paint, Clothing & Personal Property

I understand that paint may stain clothing, shoes, hair, accessories, bags, phones, or other personal belongings even when protective equipment is provided. Participants are encouraged to wear clothing and shoes that can become permanently stained.

4. Health, Allergies & Sensitivities

I am responsible for informing Party Playhouse & Events before participation of any known allergy, skin sensitivity, respiratory sensitivity, medical condition, physical limitation, or other circumstance that could affect safe participation.

5. Assumption of Risk

I understand that participation involves inherent and other risks, including slips, trips, falls, paint contact, irritation, allergic reactions, contact with equipment or other participants, and minor cuts, bruises, strains, or similar injuries.

6. Release & Waiver of Liability

This agreement is not intended to release any person or entity from liability that cannot legally be waived under Missouri law.

7. Medical Emergencies

If an injury or medical emergency occurs, I authorize Party Playhouse & Events staff to contact emergency medical services when staff reasonably believe assistance is necessary.

8. Minor Participants

If signing for a participant under 18 years old, I certify that I am the participant’s parent or legal guardian, or otherwise have legal authority to provide the consent contained in this agreement.
